About MUST

Founded in 2000, the Macau University of Science and Technology (MUST) is one of Macao's leading comprehensive universities, offering bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes with a strong emphasis on research and industry collaboration.

Mission

To cultivate talent with an international outlook and professional excellence, and to advance regional technological innovation and cultural development.

Campus & facilities

Located in Macao, the campus features modern teaching buildings, a library, laboratory centres and sports facilities that support hands-on learning and innovation.

Development & partnerships

MUST maintains partnerships with universities worldwide and actively participates in international research projects and student exchange programmes.

Macau University of Science and Technology

MUST · 澳门科技大学

A comprehensive, globally-oriented university in Macao, with the Faculty of Innovation Engineering, School of Business, Faculty of Law, Faculty of Hospitality and Tourism Management, Faculty of Humanities and Arts, University International College, School of Liberal Arts and the Medical Faculty.

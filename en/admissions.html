Admissions

Undergraduate

Undergraduate admission generally considers unified entrance exam results or an international track; applicants submit transcripts, recommendation letters and a personal statement. Requirements are detailed in the current admissions bulletin.

Postgraduate

Postgraduate applicants submit a bachelor's degree, transcripts, a research proposal (or personal statement) and two recommendation letters; some programmes require an interview or written test.

International students

International students provide proof of language proficiency (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL) or take the university language test, and should arrange visas and housing in advance.
